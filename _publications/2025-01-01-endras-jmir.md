### Abstract (Brief Summary)

- **Background:** Early neurological deterioration (END) after intravenous thrombolysis can significantly worsen outcomes in acute ischemic stroke, but reliable early-risk tools are limited.
- **Objective:** Build and validate a high-performance END prediction model to support personalized, risk-stratified post-thrombolysis management.
- **Methods:** Multicenter cohort of 1,927 patients (development: 1,361; external validation: 566); evaluated 27 clinical parameters and developed **ENDRAS** with 6 routinely available variables.
- **Results:** XGBoost-based ENDRAS achieved strong discrimination (**AUC 0.988, 95% CI 0.983–0.993**), enabled low-/high-risk triage at a 29% threshold, and was deployed as a web calculator with <0.02s inference time.
- **Conclusion:** ENDRAS translates prediction into actionable clinical pathways for targeted monitoring and intervention, with further prospective validation still needed.
