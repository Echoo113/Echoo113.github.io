### Abstract (Brief Summary)

Multivariate time series are central to modern critical infrastructure, making early anomaly prediction essential for proactive risk mitigation. MTS-JEPA addresses key limitations of standard JEPA for this setting, including representation collapse and weak sensitivity to precursor signals across temporal scales. The model combines a multi-resolution predictive objective with a soft codebook bottleneck, enabling robust latent dynamics modeling and stable optimization for early warning.

### Main Contributions

- **World Model for Anomaly Prediction:** Formulates time-series anomaly prediction as representation-space predictive modeling with JEPA-style targets, showing that latent evolution modeling is well suited for early warning.
- **Multi-Resolution JEPA:** Introduces the first multi-resolution JEPA architecture for time-series, capturing scale-varying precursors from sudden shocks to slow drifts that single-resolution models often miss.
- **Soft Codebook Bottleneck:** Adds differentiable quantization to impose a discrete inductive bias for regime transitions, while also acting as an intrinsic regularizer that improves optimization stability and mitigates collapse.
